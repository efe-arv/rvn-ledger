# Synthetic demo

These are original synthetic inputs, not the supplied assignment dataset.

`demo-try` changes plan halfway through the month: subscription charges are
`90 * 15 / 30 = 45` and `120 * 15 / 30 = 60` minor units. Its period-end plan
prices all 12 API units: 10 at 5000 micros become 5 minor units, and 2 at 15000
become 3. Five storage units at 1000 micros round half-up from 0.5 to 1 minor
unit. Subtotal is `45 + 60 + 5 + 3 + 1 = 114`; credit 5 leaves **109 TRY minor
units**.

`demo-usd` has no accepted usage. Its subscription is 30 minor units, its credit
is capped at 30, its payable total is **0**, and its remaining credit is **969**.
The later conflicting duplicate naming this account cannot contribute usage.

The nine physical lines produce 3 accepted, 1 duplicate, 1 out-of-period,
1 late and 3 quarantined decisions. The storage arrival exactly at the cutoff
is accepted; an arrival one tenth of a microsecond later is excluded.

The two expected-output files were derived from the arithmetic above. They are
compared as full JSON objects by `scripts/e2e.py` and are not regenerated by the
implementation under test.
